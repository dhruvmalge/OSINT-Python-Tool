Privacy notice

This service records analytics only when a visitor selects “Continue with analytics.” Choosing “Continue without analytics” redirects without creating a visit record.

Data collected after consent

Visit time; browser, operating system and device category inferred from the browser's User-Agent; optional screen dimensions, language and time zone; and referring hostname when supplied by the browser. If IP lookup is enabled, the visitor's IP is sent to ipwho.is to obtain approximate country, region, city, ISP and proxy/VPN/Tor indicators. The raw IP is not stored in this application's analytics database. The lookup provider has its own privacy policy and processing practices.

Retention and controls

Records older than {{ retention }} days are deleted when the dashboard is opened. Administrators can export visit records as CSV and should delete exports when no longer needed. Disable IP enrichment by setting GEO_LOOKUP=0.

Limitations

IP geolocation is approximate and can be wrong, especially with VPNs, mobile networks and corporate gateways. ISP and VPN classification may be unavailable or inaccurate. This service does not collect GPS coordinates, access a camera or microphone, or attempt to identify a person.

The site operator is responsible for providing any legally required notice, obtaining valid consent, setting a lawful retention period and responding to data requests.
